# Voice Turing Test

A blind listening test: can people tell a real human voice from an AI-generated one in Brazilian Portuguese?

Each listener hears up to 8 shuffled clips and answers "human or AI" plus a 1–5 naturalness score. No personal data is collected. Answers go to a Google Form and are analyzed against a pass criterion fixed before the test (detection not significantly above chance, binomial p ≥ 0.05, and AI naturalness within 0.5 of human).

- `manifest.json` lists clips without labels; the answer key is kept out of this repository.
- Human clips come from public datasets under open licenses (credited on the final page).

Part of [Forgeline Labs](https://github.com/forgeline-labs).
